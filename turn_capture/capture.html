Turn Capture

Stand still, then turn on the spot. The phone's gyroscope measures how far you have turned, and a photo is taken automatically at every step (for example every 30°). Each photo is saved on the lab PC with its measured angle.

Tip for 3D (VGGT): turning on one point gives almost no depth. Do 2–3 spots about 1 m apart (tap "Next spot" between them).
